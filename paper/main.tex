\begin{abstract}
Balancing competing stakeholder objectives such as accuracy, diversity, and provider fairness is a fundamental challenge in modern recommender systems. In this work, we propose a scalable framework based on Pareto Hypernetworks to continuously approximate the Pareto frontier. By conditioning a single recommendation architecture on explicit preference vectors, our approach enables real-time dynamic trade-offs without requiring repeated retraining.
\end{abstract}
